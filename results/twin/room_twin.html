    What this page is. A visualisation of the NEST decision logic placed in a room. The decision core is a JavaScript port of the firmware (pn_core.cpp / pn_node.cpp, room profile) and is checked here against the firmware's own output. Demo: people and PIR triggers are simulated by this page (seated people trigger a PIR in ≈15 % of 10 s slots; walking people always do). Replay: real occupancy labels from the Candanedo–Feldheim office dataset (UCI) with synthetic PIR events; people drawn on the plan are illustrative only. Live: state streamed from a connected ESP32 over USB (Chrome/Edge, page opened as a local file). The room layout, fixture counts and wattages are placeholders until replaced with the real DKM-block room. Nothing on this page is a measured energy result.
